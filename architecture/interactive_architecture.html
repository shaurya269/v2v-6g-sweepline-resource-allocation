How the V2V 6G Sweep-Line Allocator is built

A highway V2V simulation: a sweep-line algorithm finds nearby vehicle pairs, mmWave/THz path-loss models score each link, a greedy allocator assigns 12 channels to maximize SINR, and a small Random Forest model predicts throughput — plus an animated visualization whose "live" numbers are honestly illustrative, not a live re-run of the real algorithm.
